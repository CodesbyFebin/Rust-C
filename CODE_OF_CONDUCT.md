# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation welcoming for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Our Standards
- Be kind, specific, and constructive. Criticize code, not people.
- No harassment, hate, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Respect differing viewpoints. Disagree with evidence, not insults.

## Enforcement
Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub. Maintainers may warn, hide, or ban for violations. No retaliation for good-faith reports.
